Machine learning is a type of artificial intelligence that enables computers to learn from data without explicit programming. It is divided into six core types: Supervised Learning (predicts new data), Unsupervised Learning (finds patterns in unlabeled data), Reinforcement Learning (learns through trial and error for decision-making tasks), Semi-Supervised Learning (uses a combination of labeled and unlabeled data), and Self-Supervised Learning (learns from unlabeled data without explicit supervision).
